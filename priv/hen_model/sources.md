- **full**: the full text or PDF was read (local copy in `ref/` where noted)
- **abstract**: only the abstract or a verified bibliographic record was read
- **secondary**: the figures were checked through another document that quotes the source
- **exists**: the bibliographic record was confirmed, but no figures were taken from the source

## Not obtained / not used
- `efc_data/` (README.md, sources.md, equations.md, breed_performance.csv, breed_nutrients.csv, predict.py) isn't on GitHub `main`. It's probably in the local commits on pop-os that haven't been pushed. Nothing from it was used.
- Sakomura et al. 2015 (S9) Table 10, the UNESP digestible Lys / M+C / Thr a and b coefficients for layers. The full text was blocked (Cloudflare) and the CABI chapter was also blocked. Numbers shown in search-engine summaries were **not** used.
- Nonis & Gous 2008, *SAJAS* 38:75–82 (Thr and Lys maintenance). The full text was blocked, and the value quoted in a search summary wasn't used.
- Peguri & Coon 1993 full text (hen feather-cover equation) and Marsden & Morris 1987 (heat-stress intake). Both were blocked or not accessed, so the affected terms are marked PLACEHOLDER or ASSUMED.
